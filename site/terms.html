Terms — yourIntelligent

Updated November 9, 2026. The add-on is in development and is not publicly installable yet.

yourIntelligent is an independent writing-support project by John Phillip Custodio for university students. It provides AI questions and feedback, which can be incorrect or unhelpful. You decide what to use and remain responsible for your writing and compliance with your course's rules.

Use only content you have permission to share. Do not submit confidential or sensitive material you cannot share with the configured provider. Do not use the service to misuse another person's account or credentials.

Bring your own Gemini API key and follow the provider's terms. Provider charges and usage limits apply to your account. The app does not promise that AI requests will be free or always available. Do not send your key to the publisher or include it in support messages.

The product aims to support authorship through student-controlled feedback. It does not guarantee improved learning, ownership, grades, confidence, mental health or any legal ownership outcome.

Features and availability may change. You may stop using the app at any time, clear the session key, revoke account access or uninstall. See the privacy page for how app data and provider processing differ.

Questions: contact John Phillip Custodio.
