Common issues

  • No passage captured: select plain text outside tables and images, then capture again. The app never falls back to reading the full document.
  • Google authorization required: reopen the add-on and review the requested permissions. Managed university accounts may restrict add-ons.
  • Key rejected: use a Gemini API key, not an API endpoint URL. Check account permissions; enter the key only in the masked sidebar field.
  • Usage limit reached: check provider quota and billing settings. The app cannot increase your quota.
  • Request outcome unknown: the call may have consumed provider quota. No automatic retry was sent. Choose whether to try again.
  • Feedback feels wrong: change the focus, redirect it, skip it or keep writing. A question is a suggestion, not a judgment about your experience.

The prepared sample works without an AI key. Refresh captures locally; only an explicit reading request sends text to the provider.
