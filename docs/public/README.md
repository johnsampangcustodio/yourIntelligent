# yourIntelligent

Start with your words. Revise with a reader in the margin.

yourIntelligent is a Google Docs revision partner for university students writing personal essays and course reflections. Choose a passage and a focus. Receive one AI observation and one question. Decide what matters, then write the revision yourself.

## How it works

1. Select plain text in your document and capture it in the sidebar.
2. Review the exact passage. Choose clarity, depth, voice, or your own focus. Course context is optional.
3. Explicitly ask Gemini for a reading. A key is required only for live AI requests.
4. Keep, reject, redirect or skip the feedback. Revise directly in Docs.
5. Select your revised passage and refresh the local preview. Ask again when ready. This reading assesses only the refreshed passage.

You do not have to answer the AI or accept its suggestion to continue. The app does not insert assignment prose into your document.

## Availability and setup

Public installation is not available yet. The founder has verified capture and a Gemini reading through an Editor add-on test deployment. Marketplace review and installation from a second account remain outstanding.

After public release, install from the verified Marketplace listing, open the add-on through Docs' Extensions menu, and enter your own Gemini API key in **Key & privacy**. No Marketplace link is provided until it exists. **Try sample** uses prepared feedback without a provider call.

There is no app subscription in the current implementation. Gemini account limits and API charges may apply. A free API tier is not a guarantee of free use. Check your account before sending.

Personal reflections may contain sensitive information. Send only material you are comfortable sharing with the provider and follow your course's AI rules. Questions and feedback can be mistaken; you decide what to use.
